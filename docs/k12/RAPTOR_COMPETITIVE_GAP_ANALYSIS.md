# NexCort iQ K–12 — Raptor Technologies Competitive Gap Analysis

**Last updated:** November 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify where NexCort iQ K–12 leads, matches, or lags Raptor Technologies (the K-12 market leader in visitor management and school safety software) to inform roadmap prioritization.

---

## Raptor Technologies Overview

Raptor Technologies is the dominant K-12 school safety software vendor in the United States, used by 60,000+ schools. Their product suite includes:

- **Raptor Visitor Management** — sex offender check, ID scan, badge printing
- **Raptor Volunteer Management** — background check status tracking, clearance expiry
- **Raptor Emergency Management** — reunification, drill tracking, accountability
- **Raptor Student Safety** — BTAM integration, threat assessment workflows
- **Raptor Attendance** — daily student check-in/out

They acquired key competitors and built through platform consolidation. Their primary moat is the **installed base** (60K+ schools) and **district IT integrations** (PowerSchool, Infinite Campus, Skyward, Aeries).

## NexCort iQ Differentiators (Sustainable Advantages)

These are features Raptor does not have and cannot easily replicate:

### 1. QR/NFC Anonymous Reporting
Students and bystanders can submit anonymous safety reports by scanning a QR code without an app or login. This creates incidents directly in the dispatch queue with location context. Raptor's focus is on staff-facing check-in workflows; anonymous reporting is a gap they haven't closed.

### 2. AI-Powered Incident Triage
AWS Bedrock integration provides automatic incident type classification, EAP matching, and severity assessment. Raptor has basic form-based classification; NexCort iQ goes further with AI.

### 3. Rapid Vision (Camera AI)
Integration with campus camera feeds and AWS Rekognition for scene intelligence. No K-12 safety vendor offers this level of real-time visual AI. This is a 2–3 year ahead advantage.

### 4. War Rooms (Collaborative Incident Command)
Real-time multi-party collaborative workspace during major incidents. No K-12 vendor has this feature; it's borrowed from NexCort iQ's PSAP/emergency management DNA.

### 5. Bluelight Network Integration
Bluelight pole activation and monitoring. Raptor is visitor management focused; campus infrastructure integration is a NexCort iQ strength.

### 6. RC Translate (Multi-Language)
Real-time translation for non-English speaking reporters and families. Important in diverse districts (especially CA, TX, FL, NY).

### 7. PSAP/CAD Integration Path
Because NexCort iQ spans the PSAP and campus safety verticals, campus incidents can eventually integrate with 911 dispatch systems. No K-12 safety vendor has this pathway.

## Priority Recommendations for Sales-Readiness

Based on this analysis, here is the minimum feature set to be **competitive** in K-12 district RFPs:

### Must-have before district RFPs (Priority 1)
1. **Volunteer management** — basic registry + background check status tracking
2. **Digital badge printing** — standard expectation in every demo
3. **Visitor sign-out** — required for active visitor tracking
4. **SIS integration (PowerSchool or Clever CSV)** — required for pilot districts 100+ students

### Strong differentiator (Priority 2)
5. **Behavioral threat assessment** — growing district requirement post-Uvalde; differentiates from Raptor at a capability level
6. **Drill tracking** — often a state compliance requirement; Raptor does this; we must too
7. **Student reunification** — every K-12 RFP asks for this

### Market expansion (Priority 3)
8. **Parent/guardian notification** — required for large district deployments
9. **Evacuation roll call** — natural complement to reunification
10. **Full SIS sync** (PowerSchool native + Infinite Campus)
