# Silent Text Link

Use Silent Text when a caller cannot speak safely. They receive an SMS with a one-time secure link that opens a text session directly in your workspace. No app download required on their end.

## When to use it

- Domestic situations where speaking could escalate danger
- A caller who is hiding and cannot make noise
- Any call where the caller signals they cannot talk but needs to communicate

## How to send a Silent Text Link

1. Open the incident in your workspace.
2. Scroll to **SILENT TEXT LINK** in the right panel.
3. Confirm the caller's mobile number in E.164 format — example: +14045551234.
4. Click **Send Silent Text Link**.
5. The caller receives a plain SMS with a one-time secure link.
6. When they open the link, a text thread appears in your workspace automatically.

## Reading the text session

Messages appear in real time in the SILENT TEXT LINK panel. You can respond directly — the caller sees your replies in their browser. Every message is logged to the incident automatically.

## Session audit trail

Every Silent Text session — the link send, each message, and the session close — is written to the incident audit log. You do not need to take any additional action to preserve the record.

## If the SMS does not deliver

- Confirm the number is a mobile number, not a landline or VOIP.
- Ask the caller to check for SMS delivery if they can.
- If delivery fails, use the **Caller Video Assist** link instead, which uses the same SMS delivery path.

## Closing a session

Sessions close automatically when the caller navigates away or after a configurable timeout. You can also close the session manually from the panel.
