## Narration (HeyGen)

This walkthrough is for Venue Guest Services.

Your console is the guest-reports inbox. Guests may report through Q-R, N-F-C, S-M-S, or staff entry. You help them get the right venue response — directions, lost child process, facility issues, non-emergency security contact per SOP.

You are not a nine-one-one emergency dispatch system. That statement appears on every Guest Services page. If a guest describes an emergency in progress — chest pain, violence, fire, active threat — call nine-one-one or radio your venue security and supervisor immediately. Do not treat the inbox as CAD.

You will not see PSAP incident tables, CAD entry, or campus counseling tools. That absence is intentional. Stay inside Guest Reports. Hand security incidents to Venue Security or Supervisor. Document what you did.

Open Staff Guide for unlimited training, including When to call nine-one-one and your position article. Great guest service includes knowing when to stop being “guest services” and start public safety.
