## Narration (HeyGen)

This lesson is the most important Nexiq skill you will learn.

Nexiq is for non-emergency intake only. When a caller discloses an emergency — or the Safety Engine detects one — the product must fail safe.

Here is what should happen. A-I talk stops immediately. The phone system transfers the original caller to the City-designated live answering point. You do not continue the non-emergency interview. You do not collect more form fields. You do not “just finish this question.”

In the session UI, confirm the transfer completed. If telephony fails, treat it as priority one. Escalate to your Nexiq supervisor and use the backup voice path your agency published.

Practice this in the Demo runner using a seeded mid-call emergency scenario before you take live traffic. Know the transfer destination for your city. Know who to page when the transfer misbehaves.

Remember: Nexiq never replaces nine-one-one, CAD, or dispatcher authority. Emergency transfer exists so a non-emergency bot never traps a caller who needs a live telecommunicator.

When in doubt, get the caller to a human on the emergency path. That is success.
