| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Audience | Dispatcher, Supervisor, Agency Admin/IT |

| Tone | Orientation, shift-start checklist |

## On-screen cues

1. Sign-in → agency jurisdiction dashboard
2. Left: incident queue
3. Center: transcript
4. Right: intelligence / triage / actions
5. Connections strip → **live**
6. Help button in header

## Narration (HeyGen)

Welcome to the NexCort i-Q dispatcher workspace.

NexCort i-Q enhances emergency communications. It does not replace CAD, telephony, dispatchers, or medical direction. A-I text is a suggestion. Your agency SOP and your supervisor win if they disagree.

After you sign in at the agency URL I-T gave you — not a bookmark to another tenant — complete M-F-A when prompted.

Look at your layout. On the left is the incident queue. Select the call you are working. In the center is the transcript — live or attached — including original-language lines when multilingual is on. On the right is intelligence: triage snapshot, protocol coaching, and dispatch actions.

Before you work real calls, check the Connections strip. A-P-I should show live. If it does not, stop and escalate to Agency I-T or your supervisor.

Every shift: sign in at the correct agency URL, complete M-F-A, confirm Connections is live, and treat A-I as decision support — not orders.

Open Help any time from the header. Training access is unlimited. Use the article list for Silent Text, caller video, Pinpoint, CAD entry, translation, and notes.

You now know the map of your workspace. Next, walk a first incident from intake toward CAD.
