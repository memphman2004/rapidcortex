## Narration (HeyGen)

This lesson covers two caller-media tools: Caller Video Assist and NexiQ Pinpoint. Both are assistive. Neither replaces your location protocol or CAD authority.

Caller Video Assist sends an S-M-S link so the caller can stream live video from their phone into your workspace. Use it when seeing the scene improves triage and the caller can safely participate — smoke, injury, vehicle description, obvious hazards. Do not push video in domestic violence, active assailant, or hiding situations where lifting a phone creates danger. Prefer Silent Text or voice-only coaching instead.

To start video: select the incident, open Caller Video Assist, confirm the mobile number in E-dot-one-six-four, send the link, and coach Allow Camera. Capture notes for CAD. Close when you have what you need.

Pinpoint asks the caller’s phone for a G-P-S fix when the module is on. Use it when A-L-I is weak, the caller is mobile, or the address is unclear. Send the location request, coach Allow Location, then read the map pin. Verify against what the caller says and against CAD or A-L-I. Indoor pins — malls, decks, hospitals — can be wrong. Large accuracy circles mean “assistive,” not survey grade.

If S-M-S fails, confirm mobile, resend once per policy, then fall back to spoken location. Declines are audited. Document and continue.

These tools help you see and locate. Your SOP still decides what goes to CAD.
