## Narration (HeyGen)

Welcome to NexCort i-Q Venue Operations.

This console is event and facility operations intelligence — guest reports, section incidents, cameras for awareness, and Q-R or N-F-C posting points. It is not a nine-one-one emergency dispatch system. Say that out loud on day one, especially if you work Guest Services. Every Guest Services page must make that clear.

When life safety is in play — medical emergency, violence in progress, fire, active threat — call nine-one-one. Use the venue console to document and coordinate after you have started the public-safety path your SOP requires.

Venue Admin configures staff, zones, and Q-R. Supervisors run section status and cameras. Security works section incidents and guest-report intake. Operators support Q-R posting and field assist. Guest Services works the guest-reports inbox — not a CAD queue.

Cameras and Camera A-I are for awareness. A-I never creates an incident or dispatches units by itself.

Open Staff Guide for unlimited training: overview, When to call nine-one-one, and your position article. Protect guests by knowing the boundary between venue ops and nine-one-one.
