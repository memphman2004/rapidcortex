## Narration (HeyGen)

Welcome to NexCort i-Q Campus Safety.

This product is campus public-safety intelligence. It helps campus safety, dispatch, counseling, and administrators see incidents, buildings, Q-R locations, and cameras for the campuses in your tenant.

It is not a nine-one-one or PSAP CAD console. It is not a replacement for campus police radio, telephony, or medical direction. It is not an automatic door-lockdown system. Clery tools may hold a review queue — they do not file Timely Warnings or Clery reports for you.

When a true emergency is in progress, call nine-one-one. NexCort i-Q can hold the campus report and, where policy allows, record an escalation. It does not replace the PSAP.

Here is how roles work together. Campus Admin configures users, settings, Q-R and N-F-C, buildings, and Clery configuration. Campus Supervisor oversees live campus ops and handoff. Campus Security works field incidents and public Q-R reports. Campus Dispatch works the campus incident queue — not nine-one-one CAD entry. Campus Counselor sees the wellness queue only — no cameras, no command. Campus Faculty submit and track safety reports.

Open Staff Guide from your sidebar. Training access is unlimited. Read When to call nine-one-one, then your position article, before your first solo shift.

Campus Safety makes the campus smarter. Nine-one-one still saves lives in progress.
