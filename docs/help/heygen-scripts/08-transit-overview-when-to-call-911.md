## Narration (HeyGen)

Welcome to NexCort i-Q Transit Operations.

This product covers fleet, routes, stations, on-system incidents, cameras for awareness, and passenger report codes. It is not a replacement for nine-one-one or for your agency’s transit CAD of record.

When a life-safety emergency is in progress on a vehicle or at a station, call nine-one-one first. In NexCort i-Q, flagging a nine-one-one escalate is audit-only. There is no CAD write-back from this console unless your contract and change control explicitly say otherwise — and the platform defaults fail-closed.

Transit Admin owns users, Q-R and N-F-C, cameras, and system configuration. Supervisors run the ops dashboard, incidents, and broadcasts. Security creates field incidents on routes and at stations. Operators work from their assigned vehicle: report incidents, view unit cameras when enabled, and follow alert levels.

Passenger Q-R codes create on-system reports — not automatic nine-one-one tickets. Camera A-I tips are for humans to review, never self-dispatching tickets.

Open Staff Guide — unlimited access. Read When to call nine-one-one and your position article before your first solo shift. Keep riders safe by using the right system for the moment: transit ops for on-system work, nine-one-one for emergencies in progress.
