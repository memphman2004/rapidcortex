# 10 — Supervisor floor monitoring & CAD approval

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Video ID | `psap-supervisor-floor` |
| Audience | Supervisor, Agency Admin |
| Target length | 6:00 |
| Tone | Oversight; fail-closed honesty |

## On-screen cues

1. Supervisor floor / live view
2. SUPERVISOR WATCHING indicator
3. CAD approval queue — empty when write-back off
4. Approve / reject with reason
5. Wellness flags acknowledge

## Narration (HeyGen)

This training is for supervisors who oversee the floor — not for working every call as a primary dispatcher.

Open your supervisor live or floor view. Watch dispatcher status, open incidents, and queue load. Click into an incident when you need detail or coaching context. If your agency enables supervisor watch of a live session, expect a Supervisor Watching indicator. Monitoring is audited. Do not sit silently on a call outside that design.

Use transfer and takeover only when policy allows, and brief the receiving seat.

CAD approval appears only when CAD write-back is enabled and your agency requires supervisor approval. Open the payload. Compare location, nature, and comments to the call. Approve only when they match. Reject with a clear reason the dispatcher can fix. If write-back is off — the default, fail-closed state — the approval queue stays empty by design. Dispatchers still enter CAD in their native system. Never invent an approval to force write-back.

Acknowledge wellness flags per SOP. Pair Q-A scorecards with coaching notes. Run shift reports when command needs them.

You protect the floor with visibility and judgment — not by turning supervisor tools into a second CAD.
