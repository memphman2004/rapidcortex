## On-screen cues

3. Create incident from vehicle
4. Cameras on assigned unit (if enabled)
5. Alert level / broadcast banner
6. Call 911 for life safety

## Narration (HeyGen)

This is your Transit Operator shift briefing.

Sign in to the operator console your Transit Admin assigned. Confirm your vehicle or assignment. You work on-system incidents from here. You typically do not administer users or the system-wide Q-R registry.

During the shift: watch supervisor broadcasts and alert levels. Report incidents that happen on your vehicle or that you are assigned to handle. Use cameras on your assigned unit when enabled — for awareness only. Camera A-I does not dispatch for you.

Your agency radio and nine-one-one procedures still win. If a rider is in immediate danger, call nine-one-one. NexCort i-Q can document the on-system report. Escalation flags are audit-only. There is no automatic write-back to nine-one-one CAD from this app.

At end of shift, hand off open items per SOP and sign out on shared devices. Ask Admin for Staff Guide onboarding if you did not get overview and When to call nine-one-one in week one.

Drive safe. Report clear. Call nine-one-one when life safety will not wait.
