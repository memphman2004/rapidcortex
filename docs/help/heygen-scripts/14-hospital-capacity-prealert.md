## Narration (HeyGen)

Welcome to the NexCort i-Q hospital portal.

This surface manages capacity, E-M-S pre-alerts, and — for admins — M-C-I planning. It is not a nine-one-one dispatch console and not a CAD client.

Hospital Staff: at shift start, open Capacity for your department. Enter available beds and status truthfully — holds, cleaning, diversion. Save immediately after changes. Hand off ownership at shift change. Stale green or red status is an operational risk for routing partners.

When pre-alerts arrive, open Pre-alerts, read the inbound notice, acknowledge per hospital SOP, notify receiving clinicians, and update capacity if the alert will consume beds. Escalate ambiguous or mass-casualty alerts to Hospital Admin.

Hospital Admin: maintain the hospital profile, department capacity ownership, pre-alert handling, and M-C-I surge plans aligned with regional trauma protocols. Drill so staff can update under stress.

Pre-alerts are hospital coordination messages — not CAD incidents inside NexCort i-Q. Keep numbers honest. Keep acknowledgments timely. That is how this portal helps patients move safely.
