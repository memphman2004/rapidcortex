## Narration (HeyGen)

Welcome to Nexiq operator training.

Nexiq handles designated non-emergency calls with A-I assistance. It is not a nine-one-one dispatch console, not CAD, and not a replacement for telecommunicators. Every Nexiq page shows that banner for a reason. Believe it.

At the start of your shift, sign in at the Nexiq operator URL your admin gave you. Confirm you are in the correct agency tenant. Then open Live calls.

The live list shows active A-I sessions. Select a session to see the transcript, the caller’s intent, and the confidence level. Your job is to watch for trouble — long loops, confused callers, low confidence, or any sign the conversation should become human.

When policy says a human should finish the intake, choose Take over. Greet the caller as a live agent for your agency — not as the A-I. Complete the non-emergency intake, or transfer per your SOP. Document why you took over: low confidence, caller request, system fault, or escalating concern.

If the Safety Engine declares an emergency, do not keep interviewing for a noise complaint or abandoned vehicle. That path is covered in the emergency transfer video. Talk stops, and the original caller leg must move to the live answering point.

Never invent CAD write-back from this console. CAD create and update stay fail-closed until your agency authorizes them in writing.

Takeover is expected. Fighting to keep a caller on the bot is an operational failure. Use Help any time — training access is unlimited.

You are ready to monitor live Nexiq sessions with confidence and judgment.
