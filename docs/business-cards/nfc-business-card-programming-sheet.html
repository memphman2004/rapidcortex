Set Up Your NFC Business Card

NexCort iQ employees · finish My Contact Card first, then write the chip with our app or Chrome on Android

Employee guide · Rev 3.4
Keep with your mailed card
After your physical NFC card arrives: (1) finish My Contact Card on the NexCort iQ dashboard, then (2) write your permanent tap link onto the chip with the NexCort iQ Mobile app or Chrome on Android. Either write method works.
What gets written? Your permanent tap link — it looks like https://www.nexcortiq.us/c/ plus a short ID (example: https://www.nexcortiq.us/c/abc123). A tap opens your public contact page. Do not write the share-page link that looks like https://www.nexcortiq.us/card/yourname (QR / share only). Editing your details later does not require rewriting the chip. Step 1 · Dashboard — My Contact Card
  1. Sign in at https://app.rapidcortex.us, then open CONTACT CARD → My Contact Card in the left nav (or https://app.rapidcortex.us/rc-admin/profile/business-card; sales: https://app.rapidcortex.us/sales/business-card).
  2. On the Create tab, enter or update name, title, photo, phone, email, and links → Save Changes.
  3. Click Publish so your public page is live (status shows Published).
  4. Open the NFC tab on the same screen. Confirm NFC tag is not No tag assigned and your permanent tap link is shown (nexcortiq.us/c/…). If it says No tag assigned, ask an admin (rcadmin / rcsuperadmin) to assign a tag in CONTACT CARD → NFC Card Management before writing.
Step 2 · Write the chip — pick App or Chrome

Do this only after Step 1. If tapping the card already opens your public page, skip to the checklist.

Option A — NexCort iQ Mobile

  1. Install the app (links at bottom) if needed.
  2. Sign in with your NexCort iQ work login (rcsuperadmin, rcadmin, rcitadmin, or salescontractor).
  3. Open Business Card NFC: iPhone Settings → Marketing → Business Card NFC; or Venue/Campus Codes / Reporting Points → Business Card NFC; or iPhone agency select → Business Card NFC.
  4. Select your assigned card → Program NFC Tag.
  5. Hold the NFC area to the phone until success (Android: upper-back center; iPhone: top edge). Remove a thick case if needed.
  6. When the app confirms, status is marked programmed. Tap any phone to verify. If the write succeeded but status did not update, ask an admin to Mark programmed in NFC Card Management.

Option B — Chrome on Android (Web NFC)

  1. Open Chrome on Android only. Safari, Firefox, and all desktop browsers cannot write NFC. Turn NFC on: Settings → Connected devices → NFC → On (wording may vary by phone).
  2. Sign in at https://app.rapidcortex.us and open My Contact Card (same URLs as Step 1).
  3. Open the NFC tab → tap Write to tag. Chrome asks you to bring the tag close. (Admins can also write from CONTACT CARD → NFC Card Management.)
  4. Hold the blank NFC card flat against the upper-back center of the phone (where the NFC antenna usually sits). Hold completely still. Remove the phone case first if the write does not start — that is the most common failure.
  5. Wait for Chrome to vibrate and show success (usually 1–2 seconds): Tag written. Tap it with another phone to open the card. Do not move the card until you see it.
  6. Tap to verify: with a second phone (any phone), tap the card. It should open your public profile via the permanent tap link (nexcortiq.us/c/ + ID). Wrong page? You may have written a share link — rewrite using the tap link, not …/card/yourname.
  7. Admins only: in NFC Card Management, status may update automatically after Write to tag. If not, click Mark programmed, then Mark verified after the tap-test passes.
Chrome note: Most common failure = phone case blocking the NFC antenna. Remove the case and retry. iPhone Safari cannot write — use Option A (app) or borrow an Android for Option B.
