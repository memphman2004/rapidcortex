Program NFC Business Cards

NexCort iQ digital cards · NTAG213 / NTAG215 / NTAG216 stickers

Field programming sheet · Rev 1.1
Keep with blank tags & Android phone
What you write to the tag: https://www.nexcortiq.us/c/{cardId} — not the public profile URL /card/{slug}. Editing the card later does not require rewriting the tag.

Before you start

  • Blank NFC stickers: NTAG213NTAG215NTAG216 (all OK)
  • Android phone with NFC on — NexCort iQ Mobile preferred (RC admin → Venue or Campus)
  • Chrome on Android as a fallback (Web NFC from NFC Card Management). iPhone can read tags after programming but cannot write from Chrome/Safari.
  • Person’s digital card created and Activated / Published in Business Cards

A. Console — create & assign the tag

  1. Sign in to NexCort iQ and open NFC Card Management (Business Cards → NFC).
  2. Click Add NTAG213 (creates the permanent tag record and URL).
  3. Copy the URL shown for that card — form https://www.nexcortiq.us/c/…
  4. Choose the person under Assign to… → click Assign.

B. Write the physical sticker

Option 1 — NexCort iQ Mobile (recommended)

  1. Sign in as rcadmin or rcsuperadmin (venue/campus staff roles do not see this tool).
  2. Android: NC Venue or NC Campus with that RC account → Business Card NFC. iOS: agency select → Business Card NFC (or Reporting Points after picking an agency).
  3. Select the person’s tag and start programming.
  4. Hold the sticker to the phone until write succeeds (Android: upper back; iPhone: top edge).
  5. Status is marked programmed automatically — no separate web step.

Option 2 — Chrome on Android (fallback)

  1. On the NFC Card Management row, tap Write to tag (Web NFC) over HTTPS.
  2. Allow NFC when Chrome prompts, then hold the blank sticker flat against the upper back of the phone.
  3. Keep still until the write succeeds.
  4. Confirm status programmed (or tap Mark programmed).

C. Verify before you hand out the card

  1. With a second phone (or the same phone after leaving the write screen), tap the sticker.
  2. The phone should open the person’s public card (redirect from /c/{cardId}).
  3. Confirm name, company, and “Add to Contacts” look correct.
  4. In NFC Card Management, click Mark verified.
  5. Affix the sticker to the business card / badge holder. Do not cover with metal laminate.

Troubleshooting: App missing Business Card NFC → need an RC admin build with the feature; sign into Venue or Campus, not Safe & Sound. No write prompt in Chrome → Android Chrome only, NFC enabled, HTTPS page (Safari / desktop cannot write). Tag not detected → remove phone case; hold center of tag to NFC antenna (usually upper back). Wrong page after tap → confirm you wrote the /c/… URL, not /card/…. Lost card → Revoke the NFC id; issue a new NTAG; do not reuse revoked ids.
