# QR Codes page (Venue)

Venue **QR Codes** is the zone location list used for guest reporting (RCLI scan points), plus named report codes where your tenant uses the Field-app manager.

## Post a zone

1. Open **QR Codes**.
2. Add a location (name, building/section, zone code) or import CSV when you have many gates.
3. Download **PNG** or **PDF** and print.
4. Program NFC from the Field app (**NFC programming** on the row).
5. Test-scan before doors.

## Update or retire

- Reprint if art is damaged.
- Reprogram NFC if the tag was written to the wrong URL.
- **Deactivate** when the gate closes or the tent comes down. Scans stop; the row remains for audit.

Security may be view-only. Admin, Supervisor, and Operator can manage.

Full steps: **Update QR codes and NFC tags** and **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
