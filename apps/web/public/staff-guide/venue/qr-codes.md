# QR Codes page (Venue)

Venue **QR Codes** is the list of posted reporting points: zone scan points guests use, and named Field-app codes when this tenant uses them.

A scan or tap opens a **guest report**. It does not call 911.

## Who can change codes

- **Venue Admin, Venue Supervisor, and Venue Operator** can create, download, assign cameras, and deactivate.
- **Venue Security** can view and download when granted. Deactivate stays with the roles above unless that account was given manage.
- **Guest Services** does not manage codes. If a sign is wrong, they tell Admin or Operator.

PSAP dispatcher and supervisor accounts cannot manage venue QR or NFC.

## Post a zone

1. Open **QR Codes**.
2. Add the location (name, section, zone) or import CSV when you are posting many gates.
3. Download **PNG** or **PDF** and print the sign guests will see.
4. Program the NFC tag from the Field app. Use **NFC programming** on the row. The phone writes the tag. You do not paste a URL into the tag by hand.
5. Test-scan and test-tap before doors. Confirm the report names this section.

## Update or retire

- Reprint when the artwork is damaged or the section name changed.
- Reprogram NFC when the tag was written to the wrong destination.
- **Deactivate** when the gate closes, the tent comes down, or the section number is no longer real. Scans and taps stop. The row stays for audit.

There is no rename-in-place. To change the public name, create a new code, post it, and deactivate the old one so leftover signs stop working.

Full steps: **Update QR codes and NFC tags** and **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
