# When to call 911 (Venue)

**This is not a 911 emergency dispatch system.** Guest Services screens say so on every page because the inbox can be mistaken for a dispatch console.

## Call 911 immediately when

- Violence, weapons, or an active threat
- Fire, smoke, or a medical emergency
- A missing child when the event plan says public safety must be notified
- Any in-progress emergency your event plan assigns to 911, law enforcement, fire, or EMS

Call from a radio or phone. Then tell Supervisor or Security and update the venue incident or guest report so the rest of the building sees the same facts.

## Use NexCort iQ Venue for

- Guest reports from QR, NFC, SMS, or the inbox
- Section incidents and staff notes
- Cameras for awareness while you already know what you are looking at
- Occupant alerts inside the venue when that module is on and SOP allows it

## What the software will not do

- Place the 911 call
- Write into PSAP CAD
- Turn a Camera AI alert into a dispatched unit
- Route a Guest Services inbox item to a 911 dispatcher by itself

Guest Services: if a report sounds like an emergency, call 911 or radio Supervisor first. Do not wait for the inbox to escalate itself.

If you are unsure, make the call. A venue incident can be updated afterward. A delayed 911 call cannot.
