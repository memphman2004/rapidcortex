# When to call 911 (Venue)

**This is not a 911 emergency dispatch system.** Every guest-services screen says so for a reason.

## Call 911 immediately when

- Violence, weapons, or an active threat
- Fire or medical emergency
- A missing child when SOP requires public safety
- Any in-progress emergency your event plan assigns to 911 / law enforcement / fire / EMS

Call from a radio or phone. Then tell Supervisor / Security and update the venue incident or guest report.

## Use Rapid Cortex Venue for

- Guest reports from QR, NFC, or the inbox
- Section incidents and staff coordination
- Cameras for awareness
- Occupant alerts inside the venue when enabled

## What the software will not do

- Place the 911 call
- Write into PSAP CAD
- Turn Camera AI into automatic dispatch

Guest Services: if a report sounds like an emergency, call 911 (or radio Supervisor) first. Do not wait for the inbox workflow to “escalate itself.”
