# Zones and sections (Venue)

**Zones** are the gates, levels, clubs, and sections staff and guests use to say where something is. Incidents, guest reports, and QR posts should use the same names as the printed map and the LED wayfinding.

## Why the name matters

A guest report inherits the name on the code they scanned. If the zone still says “Section 112” after that bowl was reseated, Security goes to the wrong place.

## Keep names aligned

1. Open **Zones**.
2. Match the names on printed maps, tickets, and signage.
3. After a bowl reconfiguration or a club rename, update the zone first.
4. Then reprint QR and reprogram NFC for every post that used the old name.
5. Deactivate codes for sections that no longer exist. See **Delete locations from the dashboard**.

## Who uses zones

- **Admin** maintains the list with settings and QR.
- **Supervisor and Security** pick a zone on an incident and filter cameras by it.
- **Operator** posts codes against these names. Do not invent a section name on a sign that is not in the zone list.
- **Guest Services** reads the zone on an incoming report. They do not edit the list.

If two names exist for the same gate (“Gate A” and “A Gate”), pick one, update the zone, and deactivate the extra code.
