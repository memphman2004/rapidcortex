# Venue — new employee onboarding

Welcome to NexCort iQ Venue. **Training is unlimited** for every staff account. Open this guide as often as you need. There is no seat cap, session limit, or article-view quota.

You are not in 911 Help. You will not see CAD submission, silent text, or dispatcher SOP protocol here.

## Before your first shift

1. Sign in at the venue URL your admin sent. Do not bookmark another venue’s code.
2. Complete MFA when prompted. Privileged accounts are required to use an authenticator app.
3. Confirm the header says **NexCort iQ Venue**. Guest Services also sees **NOT A 911 EMERGENCY DISPATCH SYSTEM** on every page.
4. Open **Staff Guide** and read **Venue Operations overview**, **When to call 911**, and **your position**.
5. Learn how guests reach you: posted QR, NFC tap, Guest Reports inbox, or staff entry.

## Day one by position

- **Venue Admin** — confirm staff accounts, zones, and which QR/NFC posts are active for this event.
- **Venue Supervisor** — walk section status, guest reports, and cameras before doors.
- **Venue Security** — know your sections and how a guest report becomes a section incident.
- **Venue Operator** — print and test one QR and one NFC before doors. Incidents are view-only.
- **Guest Services** — stay in the Guest Reports inbox. You will not see cameras, QR management, or a security queue.

## When to call 911

Violence, fire, medical emergency, a missing child when SOP requires it, or any in-progress public-safety emergency: **call 911**. Then tell Supervisor or Security and update the venue incident so the rest of the staff share the same picture. This product does not place that call and does not write into PSAP CAD.

## First event

- Walk your sections. Test one QR scan and one NFC tap before doors. A code that opens the wrong section sends guests to the wrong team.
- Guest Services: practice the inbox only. Do not look for a dispatch queue.
- Operators: post codes at the gates and clubs that are open tonight. Deactivate leftover event codes after load-out.
- After the event, temporary tents, closed clubs, and last season’s section numbers should be deactivated so overnight scans stop.

## Where to come back

Staff Guide stays under **STAFF RESOURCES**. You can read every position in this vertical, including jobs you do not hold, so you know who to call on shift.
