# Venue — new employee onboarding

Welcome to Rapid Cortex Venue. **Training is unlimited** for every staff account.

You are not in 911 Help. You will not see CAD submission or dispatcher SOP protocol here.

## Day one

1. Sign in at the venue URL from your admin.
2. Confirm branding: **Rapid Cortex Venue** and the guest-services disclaimer where shown: **NOT A 911 EMERGENCY DISPATCH SYSTEM**.
3. Open **Staff Guide** and read **Venue Operations overview** plus your position.
4. Learn how guests report: posted QR, NFC tap, or Guest Reports inbox.

## When to call 911

Violence, fire, medical emergency, missing child per SOP, or any in-progress public-safety emergency: **call 911**. Then update the venue incident so operations staff share the same picture.

## First event

- Walk your sections and test one QR and one NFC before doors.
- Guest Services: practice the inbox only — do not hunt for a dispatch queue.
- Operators: print and post codes; deactivate leftover event codes after load-out.
