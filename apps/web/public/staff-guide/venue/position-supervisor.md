# Venue Supervisor

You run the event: section status, guest reports, cameras, and QR/NFC. Not a 911 supervisor floor.

## You can

- Monitor incidents and guest reports
- Update section threat / status per SOP
- Manage QR / NFC (create and deactivate)
- Request and view cameras; Camera AI never dispatches units

## Typical event

1. Confirm posts at gates and concourses scan to the right section.
2. Work guest reports with security.
3. Escalate true emergencies by calling 911, then keep the venue record current.
4. After the event, deactivate temporary codes (merch tents, closed clubs).
