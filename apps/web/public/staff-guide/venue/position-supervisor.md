# Venue Supervisor

You run the event floor: section status, guest reports, cameras, and QR/NFC. You are not a 911 supervisor and you do not sit a PSAP console.

## You can

- Monitor and update section incidents
- Work guest reports with Security and Guest Services
- Create and deactivate QR and NFC codes
- View cameras and the video wall
- Update section status per SOP

## You cannot

- Use this console as CAD or medical direction
- Open PSAP silent-text or dispatcher protocol tools
- Rely on Camera AI to create the incident or send units

## Before doors

1. Confirm posts at gates and concourses scan and tap to the right section.
2. Know which cameras cover those sections.
3. Confirm Guest Services is in the inbox and knows to call 911 rather than wait on a software route.

## During the event

Work guest reports that need a section response. Keep incident notes factual. Call 911 for in-progress emergencies, then update the venue record so the next supervisor sees what was already done.

## After the event

Deactivate temporary codes: merch tents, closed clubs, one-night gates. A sign left on a barricade will keep creating reports.
