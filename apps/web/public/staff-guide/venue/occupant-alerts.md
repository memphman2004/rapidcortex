# Occupant alerts (Venue)

**Occupant Alerts** notifies people in the venue when the module is on. It is not 911 and not a replacement for the venue’s public-address or fire-alarm systems.

## Use it for

- Weather delay, all-clear, gate hold, or other SOP occupant notices

## Do not use it instead of

- Calling 911
- Fire alarm / life-safety systems
- Your contracted emergency notification vendor, if SOP requires that vendor

## Send

1. Open **Occupant Alerts**.
2. Pick a template or compose per SOP.
3. Send. Note the send on the related incident.

Recipient and template management requires the manage-recipients permission.
