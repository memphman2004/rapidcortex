# Occupant alerts (Venue)

**Occupant Alerts** notifies people in the venue when the module is turned on for this tenant. It is not 911. It is not a replacement for the public-address system, the fire alarm, or a contracted emergency-notification vendor.

## Use it for

- Weather hold, all-clear, gate hold, or another occupant notice your SOP already allows in this channel

## Do not use it instead of

- Calling 911
- The fire alarm or other life-safety systems
- The venue PA when SOP says the PA is the occupant channel
- A contracted mass-notification vendor when the event plan names that vendor

## Send

1. Open **Occupant Alerts**.
2. Choose the template your SOP approved, or write the notice in the form SOP allows.
3. Confirm who will receive it before you send.
4. Send, then note the send on the related venue incident so the next shift knows the message went out.

Managing recipients and templates requires the manage-recipients permission. If you can read alerts but cannot edit the list, ask Venue Admin.

Guest Services does not send occupant alerts from the inbox.
