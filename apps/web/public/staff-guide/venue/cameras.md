# Cameras, Camera AI, and video wall (Venue)

Venue cameras support **game-day awareness**. Camera AI **never creates an incident and never dispatches units**.

Guest Services does not have camera navigation.

## Cameras

1. Open **Cameras**.
2. Filter by section when the registry includes section tags.
3. Use live view for the incident you are working.

## Camera AI

Treat alerts as tips. If something is real, create or update a **venue incident**, or call 911.

## Video wall

Open **Video Wall** for a multi-camera event picture. It is not caller-video 911 assist.

## Tie cameras to a code

Admin, Supervisor, and Operator can **Assign cameras** on a QR row so a guest scan opens the right views. See **Update QR codes and NFC tags**.
