# Cameras, Camera AI, and video wall (Venue)

Venue cameras support **game-day awareness**. They do not replace radio, and they do not dispatch anyone.

**Camera AI never creates an incident and never dispatches units.** An AI alert is a tip. A person decides what to do.

Guest Services does not have camera navigation. That is intentional.

## Cameras

1. Open **Cameras**.
2. Filter by section when the registry is tagged by gate, level, or club.
3. Use live view for the incident you are already working. Do not treat an unlabeled camera as proof of a section.

## Camera AI

If an alert matches something you can confirm, create or update a **venue incident**, or call 911 when the event plan says to. If you cannot confirm it, say so in the note. Do not close an emergency because the alert stopped.

## Video wall

Open **Video Wall** for a multi-camera picture of the event. It is not 911 caller video and it is not a PSAP assist session.

## Tie a camera to a posted code

Admin, Supervisor, and Operator can **Assign cameras** on a QR row so a guest scan points staff at the views for that area. See **Update QR codes and NFC tags**.

If a camera is offline, say so on the incident. Do not wait on the wall to “catch up” during an in-progress emergency — call 911 or radio Security.
