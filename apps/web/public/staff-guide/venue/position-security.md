# Venue Security

You log section incidents and act on guest reports from the floor. You are not a 911 dispatcher. This console does not give you CAD.

## You can

- Create and update section incidents
- Read and work Guest Reports that need a security response
- Use cameras and zones for awareness
- View and download QR artwork when your account allows it

## You cannot

- Submit CAD or open a 911 dispatcher workspace
- Assume you can deactivate a QR or NFC post — that is Admin, Supervisor, or Operator unless your account was granted manage
- Treat a Camera AI alert as an order to dispatch

## On the floor

1. Know your sections before doors.
2. Watch inbound guest reports for those sections.
3. For violence, fire, medical emergency, or a missing child under SOP, call 911 or follow the event radio plan first. Then write the venue incident.
4. If a posted code is wrong or dead, tell Supervisor or Operator. Do not leave guests scanning a sign that opens the wrong place.
5. Notes should say what you saw and who you notified. The next shift will read them without you there.
