# Venue Operations overview

NexCort iQ Venue is **event and facility operations intelligence**. **This is not a 911 emergency dispatch system.**

Staff Guide is the venue knowledge base. The Help tab on a 911 console is a different product and a different set of tools.

## What this console is for

- Section and gate incidents that venue staff work
- Guest reports from QR, NFC, SMS (when provisioned), or staff entry
- Posted QR and NFC codes so a report lands on the right section
- Cameras and the video wall for awareness
- Occupant alerts inside the venue when that module is on
- Staff accounts, zones, and event configuration

## This product is not

- 911 CAD, telephony, or medical direction
- A replacement for venue radio or the region’s PSAP
- Automatic law-enforcement dispatch from Camera AI
- A way for Guest Services to open a security command console

Call 911 for in-progress emergencies. Use NexCort iQ for the venue record around that event.

## Positions

- **Venue Admin** — staff, QR/NFC, zones, analytics, and settings. Not 911 dispatch.
- **Venue Supervisor** — section status, guest reports, cameras, and QR/NFC. Not a PSAP floor.
- **Venue Security** — section incidents and guest-report intake. Cameras for awareness.
- **Venue Operator** — QR/NFC posting and view-only incidents. Not incident command.
- **Guest Services** — guest reports inbox only. No security console, cameras, or CAD. Every page carries the 911 disclaimer.

Every venue staff account can read every position article. Access is unlimited.

## How a guest report moves

1. A guest scans a posted code, taps NFC, or texts the venue number.
2. The report opens in **Guest Reports** with the section named on that code.
3. Guest Services handles non-emergency guest issues. Security and Supervisor take anything that needs a section response.
4. If it is an in-progress emergency, staff call 911. The software record is updated after that call. It does not replace it.

## Guest Assist and QR

Posted QR and NFC codes open a guest report — not a 911 call. Keep codes accurate so the report lands on the right section. See **Update QR codes and NFC tags** and **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
