# Venue Operations overview

Rapid Cortex Venue is **event and facility operations intelligence**. **This is not a 911 emergency dispatch system.**

Staff Guide (this portal) is the venue knowledge base. The purple **Help** tab on 911 consoles is a different product.

## This product is not

- 911 CAD, telephony, or medical direction
- A replacement for venue radio or the region’s PSAP
- Automatic law-enforcement dispatch from Camera AI

Call 911 for in-progress emergencies. Use Rapid Cortex for section incidents, guest reports, QR/NFC posts, and cameras.

## Positions

- **Venue Admin** — staff, QR/NFC, zones, analytics, settings. Not 911 dispatch.
- **Venue Supervisor** — section status, guest reports, cameras, QR/NFC. Not PSAP floor tools.
- **Venue Security** — section incidents and guest-report intake.
- **Venue Operator** — QR/NFC posting, view-only incidents. Not full incident command.
- **Guest Services** — guest reports inbox only. No security console, cameras, or CAD.

Every venue staff account can read every position article. Access is unlimited.

## Guest Assist and QR

Posted QR and NFC codes open a guest report — not a 911 call. Keep codes accurate so the report lands on the right section. See **Update QR codes and NFC tags** and **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
