# Venue Guest Services

**This is not a 911 emergency dispatch system.** Your console is the **Guest Reports** inbox.

## You can

- Read and work guest reports that arrive from QR, NFC, or staff entry

## You cannot

- Open security cameras, QR management, CAD, or incident command
- Transfer a guest into a 911 dispatcher workspace from this product

## Typical event

1. Sign in and stay in Guest Reports.
2. For lost property, directions, and non-emergency guest issues, handle per venue SOP.
3. If a report describes an in-progress emergency, **call 911** (or radio Supervisor / Security per SOP) immediately. Do not wait on software routing.

Read **Venue Operations overview** so you know who owns QR posts and security incidents.
