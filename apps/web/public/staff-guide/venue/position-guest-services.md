# Venue Guest Services

**This is not a 911 emergency dispatch system.** That line stays on every page of your console. Your job is the **Guest Reports** inbox: lost property, wayfinding, accessibility, and other guest issues the event plan assigns to guest services.

## You can

- Read guest reports from QR, NFC, SMS, or staff entry
- Add notes and mark a report handled per venue SOP
- Hand a non-emergency issue to the team SOP names

## You cannot

- Open the security incident queue
- View cameras, the video wall, or Camera AI
- Create, move, or deactivate QR and NFC codes
- Manage staff, zones, analytics, or settings
- Transfer a guest into a 911 dispatcher workspace from this product

## A shift in the inbox

1. Sign in and stay on **Guest Reports**.
2. Read the section on the report before you reply. That name came from the sign the guest used.
3. Handle non-emergency guest issues per SOP. Write a short note when you hand someone off.
4. If the words describe violence, fire, a medical emergency, or a missing child under SOP, **call 911** or radio Supervisor / Security immediately. Do not wait for the inbox to escalate itself.
5. If many reports name the same section, say they are the same event so Security is not sent once per message.

## When a sign is wrong

You cannot fix the code. Tell Venue Admin or Operator the section name guests are hitting. They deactivate and reprint. Keep helping the guest in front of you while that happens.

Read **When to call 911** and **Guest Reports inbox** before doors.
