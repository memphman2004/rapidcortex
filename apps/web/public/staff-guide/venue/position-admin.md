# Venue Admin

You own the venue tenant: staff, zones, QR/NFC, cameras, analytics, and settings. This is event operations, not a 911 PSAP. You do not land on a dispatcher workspace.

## You can

- Invite, change, and deactivate venue staff
- Create, download, assign cameras, and deactivate QR and NFC codes
- Maintain zones and sections
- Work incidents, guest reports, analytics, occupant alerts, and onboarding packets
- Open settings for this venue

## You cannot

- Run 911 CAD, silent text, or dispatcher SOP tools from this console
- Give Guest Services a security command view — their navigation is the inbox by design
- Treat Camera AI as automatic dispatch

## Before doors

1. Confirm tonight’s staff are active and last event’s contractors are not.
2. Walk the zone list against the map.
3. Confirm gate, club, and concourse codes scan to those zones. Deactivate anything from a previous configuration.
4. Assign cameras on the codes Security will need.

## During the event

Watch incidents and guest reports. When something is an in-progress emergency, the 911 call still happens on radio or phone. Update the venue record after.

## After load-out

Deactivate temporary posts. Deactivate seasonal accounts that are done. Leave the guide available — returning staff can reread it next event with no new license step.
