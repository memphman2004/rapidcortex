# Venue Admin

You own the venue tenant: staff, zones, QR/NFC, cameras, and settings. This is event operations, not a 911 PSAP.

## You can

- Manage staff accounts and venue settings
- Create, download, assign cameras, and deactivate QR / NFC codes
- Work incidents, guest reports, analytics, occupant alerts, and onboarding packets

## You cannot

- Run 911 CAD or silent-text dispatcher tools from this console
- Let Guest Services users into security command — their nav is inbox-only by design

## Locations

**QR Codes** in the venue console is the zone location list (RCLI). Deactivate codes that should no longer scan. Reprint and reprogram NFC after map or section changes. Details: **Update QR codes and NFC tags** and **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
