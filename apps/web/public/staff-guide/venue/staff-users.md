# Staff accounts (Venue Admin)

Venue users must receive venue roles only. A `dispatcher` role sends that person to 911 Help and a PSAP workspace. That is the wrong console for event staff.

## Roles

- **Venue Admin** — staff, QR/NFC, zones, analytics, settings
- **Venue Supervisor** — live ops, QR/NFC, guest reports, cameras
- **Venue Security** — section incidents and guest-report intake
- **Venue Operator** — QR/NFC posting, view-only incidents
- **Guest Services** — Guest Reports inbox only, with the 911 disclaimer on every page

Give the smallest role that matches the job. A seasonal usher lead is an Operator or Guest Services account, not an Admin.

## Invite staff

1. Open **Staff**.
2. Invite with a work email and the matching venue role.
3. Tell them to complete MFA on first sign-in.
4. Point them at **Staff Guide**: overview, their position, and **When to call 911**. Training is unlimited. They can reread it during the event.

## During the event

- Do not share one login across a gate crew. Each person needs their own account so notes and audit show who acted.
- If someone changes jobs mid-event (Guest Services moving to Security), change the role. Do not tell them to use a coworker’s session.

## Remove access

Deactivate accounts when the contractor, show crew, or seasonal staff leave. Do not leave those accounts active until next season unless that person still works the building.
