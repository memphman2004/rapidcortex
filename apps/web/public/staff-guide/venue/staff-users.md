# Staff accounts (Venue Admin)

Venue users must receive **VENUE_*** roles only. A `dispatcher` role sends them to 911 Help and a PSAP workspace.

## Roles

- **Venue Admin** — staff, QR, zones, settings
- **Venue Supervisor** — live ops, QR/NFC, guest reports, cameras
- **Venue Security** — section incidents and guest-report intake
- **Venue Operator** — QR/NFC posting, view-only incidents
- **Guest Services** — Guest Reports inbox only, with the 911 disclaimer on every page

## Invite staff

1. Open **Staff**.
2. Invite with work email and the matching venue role.
3. Send them **Staff Guide** for unlimited onboarding (overview, their position, When to call 911).

## Remove access

Deactivate accounts when the event contractor leaves. Do not leave seasonal staff active year-round unless they still work the building.
