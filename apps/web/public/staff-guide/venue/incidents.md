# Work venue incidents

**Incidents** are venue-scoped (section, gate, concourse). There is no PSAP incident table and no CAD entry.

## Open incidents

1. Sign in to the venue console (`/app/venue/{code}`).
2. Select **Incidents**.
3. Confirm orange venue branding — not a sky-blue 911 workspace.

Operators typically have **view only**. Security, Supervisor, and Admin work the queue.

## Handle an incident

1. Open the row and confirm section / zone.
2. Add notes that a later shift can use.
3. Tie guest reports to the same section when they are the same event.
4. Request cameras if your role can (see **Cameras, Camera AI, and video wall**).
5. Close when the venue response is done.

## Emergencies

Call 911, then keep the venue incident current. Escalation in-product is coordination, not CAD write-back.

## After the event

Deactivate temporary QR posts (see **Delete locations from the dashboard**) so leftover signs do not create overnight reports.
