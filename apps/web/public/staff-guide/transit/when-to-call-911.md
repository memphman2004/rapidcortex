# When to call 911 (Transit)

NexCort iQ Transit is **not** a 911 PSAP console and **not** transit CAD write-back.

## Call 911 immediately when

- Assault, weapons, or an active threat on a vehicle or platform
- Fire, smoke, or medical emergency
- A person on the right-of-way when SOP requires emergency services
- Any in-progress public-safety emergency

Then update the transit incident. Supervisors can flag **911 escalate** for audit. That flag **does not** write to CAD.

## Use NexCort iQ Transit for

- Fleet and route awareness
- On-system incident records
- Passenger QR/NFC reports
- Cameras on vehicles and stations
- Occupant alerts / broadcasts when enabled

If you are an operator on a moving vehicle, follow radio SOP for 911 — then file or update the incident when it is safe.
