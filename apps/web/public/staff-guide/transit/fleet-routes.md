# Fleet, routes, and stations (Transit)

The transit console is built around **vehicles, routes, and stations**.

## Dashboard and fleet

1. Sign in. Confirm **Transit Operations** (indigo), not a 911 workspace.
2. **Dashboard** shows system posture and open incidents.
3. **Fleet** lists vehicles. Open a vehicle for assignment, cameras, and related incidents.
4. Operators use **My vehicle** instead of the full admin fleet.

## Routes and stations

- **Routes** — in-service patterns and vehicles on them
- Stations appear with incidents and passenger codes
- Admin configures vehicles and routes under **CONFIG** (**Vehicles**, **Routes**)

## Out of service

When a bus or car leaves the roster, deactivate its passenger **QR / NFC** so leftover stickers stop collecting reports. See **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
