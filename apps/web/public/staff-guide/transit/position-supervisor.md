# Transit Supervisor

You run transit ops: incidents, alert level, broadcasts, and QR/NFC. Not a 911 supervisor.

## You can

- Work the ops dashboard, fleet, routes, and incidents
- Create and deactivate passenger QR / NFC
- Flag 911 escalation per SOP (audit only — no CAD write-back)
- Use cameras when transit cameras are enabled

## Typical shift

1. Check alert level and open incidents.
2. Confirm on-service vehicles still have working passenger codes.
3. For emergencies, call 911 and keep the transit incident updated.
4. Deactivate codes on out-of-service vehicles so leftover stickers do not collect stale reports.
