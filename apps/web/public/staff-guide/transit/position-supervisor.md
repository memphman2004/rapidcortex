# Transit Supervisor

You run day-to-day ops: incidents, alert level, broadcasts, and QR/NFC for the system.

## You can

- Work the ops dashboard and incident queue
- Create/deactivate passenger QR codes
- Send occupant alerts when enabled
- Escalate to 911 as **audit-only** — no CAD write-back

## You cannot

- Replace the PSAP or transit agency CAD of record
- Ignore the Staff Guide banner that this is not 911 dispatch

Coach Security and Operators to call 911 first when life safety is in play.
