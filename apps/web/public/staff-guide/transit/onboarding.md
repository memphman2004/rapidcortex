# Transit — new employee onboarding

Welcome to NexCort iQ Transit. **Training access is unlimited.**

This is not 911 Help. You will not see dispatcher CAD entry here.

## Day one

1. Sign in at the transit console URL from your admin.
2. Confirm **Transit Operations** branding (indigo), not a PSAP sky-blue 911 workspace.
3. Open **Staff Guide** and read the overview plus your position.
4. If you work vehicles: know your bus/rail ID and whether a passenger QR/NFC is posted onboard.

## When to call 911

In-progress threat, medical emergency, fire, or other public-safety emergency: **call 911**. Flag or escalate in NexCort iQ per SOP for audit — that is not CAD write-back.

## First week

- Operators: **My vehicle**, how to file an on-system incident.
- Security: route/station incident intake.
- Admin / Supervisor: QR Codes for vehicles and stations; deactivate retired units.
