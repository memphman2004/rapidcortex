# Transit users (Transit Admin)

Only **Transit Admin** invites Transit Admin, Supervisor, Security, and Operator accounts. Do not assign PSAP or venue roles to transit seats.

## Invite

1. Open **Users**.
2. Invite the work email and the transit role that matches the job.
3. Send **Staff Guide** — unlimited training (overview, position article, When to call 911).
4. Confirm the user lands on `/app/transit/{role}` or `/transit/{code}` — not a PSAP dashboard.

## Remove access

Deactivate the same day an operator or contractor leaves. Operators should not keep tenant-wide admin logins. Re-invite rather than sharing passwords when someone returns.
