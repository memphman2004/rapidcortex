# Transit users (Transit Admin)

Only **Transit Admin** invites Transit Admin, Supervisor, Security, and Operator accounts. Do not assign PSAP roles.

## Invite

1. Open **Users**.
2. Invite work email and the transit role that matches the job.
3. Send **Staff Guide** — unlimited training (overview, position, When to call 911).

## Remove access

Deactivate when an operator or contractor leaves. Operators should not keep tenant-wide logins.
