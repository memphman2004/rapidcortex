# Cameras and video wall (Transit)

Transit cameras (ONVIF/RTSP registry, Ring/Nest when enabled) are for **awareness**. They do not auto-create incidents.

## Cameras

1. Open **Cameras** when `transit cameras` is on for your tenant.
2. Prefer the vehicle or station you are working.
3. Operators typically see cameras on the assigned vehicle only.

## Camera AI and video wall

**Camera AI** alerts are tips, not tickets. **Video Wall** is a multi-view for supervisors and admins.

Assign cameras on a passenger QR row so a scan can open the right vehicle or station views. See **Update QR codes and NFC tags**.
