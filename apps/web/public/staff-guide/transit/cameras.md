# Cameras and video wall (Transit)

Transit cameras (ONVIF/RTSP registry, Ring/Nest when enabled) are for **awareness**. They do **not** auto-create incidents and they do **not** write to transit CAD or 911 CAD.

## Cameras page

1. Open **Cameras** when transit cameras are enabled for your tenant.
2. Prefer the vehicle or station you are working.
3. Operators typically see cameras on the assigned vehicle only.
4. Supervisors and Admins can open wider station / fleet views when policy allows.

## Camera AI and video wall

- **Camera AI** alerts are tips for a human to review — never tickets that dispatch themselves.
- **Video Wall** is a multi-view for supervisors and admins during an event.
- Assign cameras on a passenger QR row so a scan can open the right vehicle or station views.

If a camera is dark, troubleshoot registry / network with Transit Admin. Do not invent an incident solely from a failed stream.
