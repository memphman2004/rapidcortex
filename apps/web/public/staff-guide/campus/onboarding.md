# Campus — new employee onboarding

Welcome to NexCort iQ Campus Safety. **Training access is unlimited.** There is no seat, session, or article-view limit. Use this Staff Guide whenever you need it.

This is not 911 Help. You will not see CAD entry, silent text, or dispatcher SOP protocol here.

## Day one

1. Sign in at the campus URL your admin gave you. Do not bookmark another tenant’s campus code.
2. Complete MFA when prompted.
3. Confirm the header says **NexCort iQ · Campus** and that this is **not a 911 dispatch console**.
4. Open **Staff Guide**. Read **Campus Safety overview**, **When to call 911**, and **your position**.
5. Walk the pages your role actually has. If Cameras or QR Codes is missing, that role is not supposed to have it.

## Day one by position

- **Campus Admin** — confirm users, buildings, and which QR/NFC posts are active.
- **Campus Supervisor** — open incidents, cameras, and QR Codes. You can deactivate a bad post.
- **Campus Security** — incidents, cameras, and view-only QR. You cannot deactivate a code.
- **Campus Dispatch** — the campus incident queue and cameras. Not 911 CAD, and not QR management.
- **Campus Counselor** — the wellness queue only. No cameras and no security incident table.
- **Campus Faculty** — submit and track safety reports. Call 911 yourself for an in-progress emergency.

## When to call 911

Call 911, or campus PD if your SOP names that number, for in-progress violence, fire, medical emergency, or any event your emergency plan says is a public-safety emergency.

Use NexCort iQ for the campus report, QR and NFC intake, wellness routing, and coordination around that event. An escalation flag is a handoff record. It does not place the call and it does not write PSAP CAD.

## First week

- Learn how students and visitors report: posted QR and NFC, and faculty reports.
- If you manage locations, complete **Update QR codes and NFC tags** and **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
- If you are counseling staff, stay in the wellness queue. Do not look for the camera wall.
- Ask Campus Admin where occupant alerts, EAP checklists, and Clery review live for this campus. Those modules are not on for every tenant.

## Ongoing reference

Return here from **STAFF RESOURCES**. You can reread every position, including jobs you do not hold, so you know who to call on shift.
