# Campus — new employee onboarding

Welcome to Rapid Cortex Campus Safety. Use this Staff Guide whenever you need it. **Training access is unlimited** — there is no seat, session, or article-view limit.

This is not 911 Help. You will not see CAD entry, silent text, or dispatcher SOP protocol here.

## Day one

1. Sign in at the campus URL your admin gave you. Do not bookmark another tenant’s slug.
2. Complete MFA when prompted.
3. Confirm the header says **Rapid Cortex · Campus** and **NOT A 911 DISPATCH CONSOLE**.
4. Open **Staff Guide** (this page). Read **Campus Safety overview** and **your position** article.
5. Walk the dashboard: incidents, cameras (if your role has them), and QR Codes (if your role has them).

## When to call 911

Call 911 (or campus PD per local SOP) for in-progress violence, fire, medical emergency, or any event your SOP says is a public-safety emergency.

Use Rapid Cortex for campus safety reports, QR/NFC guest or student reports, wellness routing, and coordination **around** those events. Escalation to 911, where enabled, is a handoff — it is not CAD write-back.

## First week

- Learn how students and visitors report via posted QR and NFC.
- If you manage locations, complete **Update QR codes and NFC tags** and **Delete locations from the dashboard**.
- If you are counseling staff, stay in the wellness queue — you will not see the security camera wall.
- Ask your Campus Admin where occupant alerts, EAP checklists, and Clery review live for this campus.

## Ongoing reference

Return to Staff Guide from the header or **STAFF RESOURCES** in the sidebar. You can reread every position, including jobs you do not hold, so you know who to call on shift.
