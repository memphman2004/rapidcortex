# Campus Admin

Campus Admin owns the tenant: people, places, codes, and policy. You are not a 911 dispatcher and you do not land on a live PSAP workspace.

## You can

- Invite and manage Campus Supervisor, Security, Dispatch, Counselor, and Faculty
- Create, download, reassign cameras, and deactivate QR and NFC reporting points
- Configure buildings, zones, cameras, occupant alerts, and, where licensed, Clery, EAP, and war rooms
- Read analytics and reports for this tenant
- Record an escalation toward 911 per SOP

## You cannot

- Submit CAD from a 911 dispatcher workspace
- File a Clery Annual Security Report or Timely Warning as the legal filing from this product
- See another agency’s campus without an NexCort iQ internal operator login
- Put campus staff on PSAP roles so they “see more”

## Typical shift

1. Confirm site scope: All campuses, or one campus.
2. Check open incidents and unassigned QR reports.
3. Provision or deactivate staff whose jobs changed.
4. Keep QR and NFC current after construction, events, or damaged signs.
5. Review Clery queues if that module is on. If the count disagrees with campus PD, follow the Clery coordinator.

## Locations

**QR Codes** holds named Field-app codes. **Location QR (RCLI)** holds building and zone identifiers. To retire a post, deactivate it. See **Delete locations from the dashboard**. To reprint or rewrite NFC, see **Update QR codes and NFC tags**.
