# Campus Admin

Campus Admin owns the tenant: people, places, codes, and policy. You are not a 911 dispatcher and you do not land on a live PSAP workspace.

## You can

- Invite and manage Campus Supervisor, Security, Dispatch, Counselor, and Faculty accounts
- Create, download, reassign, and deactivate QR / NFC reporting points
- Configure buildings, zones, cameras, occupant alerts, and (where licensed) Clery / EAP / war rooms
- Read analytics and reports for the campus tenant
- Escalate campus incidents toward 911 per SOP — Rapid Cortex does not replace the PSAP

## You cannot

- Submit CAD from a 911 dispatcher workspace
- File Clery ASR / Timely Warning as a legal filing from this product
- See another agency’s campus without an RC internal operator login

## Typical shift

1. Confirm site scope (All campuses vs one campus).
2. Check open incidents and unassigned QR reports.
3. Provision or deactivate staff who changed jobs.
4. Keep QR/NFC posts current after construction, events, or damaged signs.
5. Review Clery queues if that module is on.

## Locations

Use **QR Codes** for named Field-app codes and **Location QR (RCLI)** for building/zone identifiers. To retire a post, deactivate it — see **Delete locations from the dashboard**. To reprint or re-tap NFC, see **Update QR codes and NFC tags**.
