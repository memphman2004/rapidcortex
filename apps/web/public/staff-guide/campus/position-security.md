# Campus Security

You take campus safety reports in the field and from QR/NFC posts. You are not a 911 dispatcher.

## You can

- Create and update campus security incidents
- Review public reports submitted via QR/NFC
- Use cameras, video wall, and zones for awareness
- Open QR Codes in **view only** — download artwork, do not deactivate

## You cannot

- Deactivate or create QR/NFC codes (ask Admin or Supervisor)
- Enter CAD or run a PSAP workspace
- Access counselor-only wellness notes outside your SOP

## Typical shift

1. Sign in and check the incident list for your campus.
2. Patrol with awareness of posted QR/NFC points — those are how many reports arrive.
3. Log incidents with building/zone when you have it.
4. For in-progress emergencies, call 911 first, then update the campus incident.

## If a posted code is wrong

Do not try to delete it from Security. Report the location to Campus Supervisor or Admin so they can deactivate and reprint. Read **Update QR codes and NFC tags** so you know what “fixed” looks like when you retest the post.
