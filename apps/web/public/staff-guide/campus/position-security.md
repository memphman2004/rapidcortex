# Campus Security

You take campus safety reports in the field and from QR and NFC posts. You are not a 911 dispatcher.

## You can

- Create and update campus security incidents
- Review public reports that arrived from QR or NFC
- Use cameras, the video wall, and zones for awareness
- Open **QR Codes** to view and download artwork

## You cannot

- Create or deactivate QR and NFC codes. Ask Admin or Supervisor
- Enter CAD or open a PSAP workspace
- Read counselor-only wellness notes outside your SOP
- Treat Camera AI as an order to dispatch

## Typical shift

1. Sign in and check incidents for your campus. Use the site switcher if the tenant has more than one.
2. Know where the posted codes are. That is how many reports arrive.
3. Log the building or zone when you have it.
4. For an in-progress emergency, call 911 first, then update the campus incident.
5. Write notes the next shift can use without calling you.

## If a posted code is wrong

Do not try to delete it. Tell Campus Supervisor or Admin the building and what the sign says. They deactivate and reprint. After they say it is off, scan it once to confirm the report no longer opens. **Update QR codes and NFC tags** describes what “fixed” looks like.
