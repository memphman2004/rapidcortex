# Campus Safety overview

Rapid Cortex Campus Safety is **campus public-safety intelligence**. It helps campus safety, dispatch, counseling, and administrators see incidents, buildings, QR locations, and cameras across every campus in the same tenant.

This Staff Guide is for campus employees. It is **not** the 911 Help tab used in a PSAP dispatch console.

## This product is not

- A 911 / PSAP CAD console
- A replacement for campus PD radio, telephony, or medical direction
- An automatic door-lockdown system
- A Clery Act filing or Timely Warning system (Clery tools support review; they do not file for you)

When a true emergency is in progress, **call 911**. Rapid Cortex can capture the campus report and, where policy allows, escalate — it does not replace the PSAP.

## How the vertical is organized

One Rapid Cortex tenant can hold every campus. Operators use **All campuses** by default and can filter to one location with the site switcher.

- **Campus Admin** — users, settings, QR/NFC, buildings, Clery configuration. Not live 911 dispatch.
- **Campus Supervisor** — ops oversight, QR/NFC, incidents, occupant alerts. Not PSAP floor monitoring.
- **Campus Security** — field incidents, QR report intake, cameras. View/download QR only.
- **Campus Dispatch** — campus incident queue and cameras. Not 911 CAD entry.
- **Campus Counselor** — wellness queue only. No cameras or command.
- **Campus Faculty** — submit safety reports. No security console.

Open **Positions** in this guide for the full playbook for each job. Every staff member can read every position article — access is unlimited.

## Daily tools

- **Dashboard** — campus posture, open incidents, not a CAD workspace
- **Incidents** — campus safety reports (counselors see the wellness queue)
- **QR Codes** — named report codes and Location QR (RCLI)
- **Cameras / Camera AI / Video Wall** — awareness only; AI never creates an incident or dispatches units
- **Zones / Buildings** — geography for reports and cameras
- **Staff Guide** (this portal) — training and reference, unlimited
