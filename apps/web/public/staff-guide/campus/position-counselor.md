# Campus Counselor

Your Rapid Cortex access is the **wellness queue**. Anonymous QR welfare and mental-health reports can route here. This is not a 911 dispatch console, not a camera wall, and not campus-wide incident command.

## You can

- Open the counseling / wellness queue
- Add counseling notes per SOP
- Use reports and translate (when enabled)

## You cannot

- Open cameras, EAP, war rooms, or QR management
- Dispatch security units or enter CAD
- See the full security incident table as a dispatcher would

## Typical shift

1. Sign in and confirm you are on **Wellness Queue**, not a security dashboard.
2. Work new welfare reports. Follow campus counseling SOP for imminent harm — that often means calling 911 or campus PD, not “closing the ticket.”
3. Do not share clinical detail in security incident comments unless SOP requires a specific handoff.

## QR reports

Students may scan a posted code to submit a welfare report. You do not manage those codes. If a code is posting to the wrong queue, tell Campus Admin.
