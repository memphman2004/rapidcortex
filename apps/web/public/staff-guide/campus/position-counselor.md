# Campus Counselor

Your access is the **wellness queue**. Welfare and mental-health reports, including some anonymous QR reports, can route here. This is not a 911 dispatch console, not a camera wall, and not campus-wide incident command.

## You can

- Open the counseling / wellness queue
- Add counseling notes per SOP
- Use reports and Translate when those are enabled for this role

## You cannot

- Open cameras, EAP, war rooms, or QR management
- Dispatch security units or enter CAD
- See the full security incident table the way Campus Dispatch does
- Send occupant alerts from this queue

## Typical shift

1. Sign in and confirm you are on the wellness queue, not a security dashboard.
2. Work new welfare reports in the order SOP requires.
3. Imminent harm follows campus counseling SOP. That often means calling 911 or campus PD. Closing the item is not that call.
4. Do not paste clinical detail into a security incident comment unless SOP names a specific handoff and what may be shared.

## QR reports

A student may scan a posted code to submit a welfare report. You do not manage those codes. If reports are landing in the wrong queue or naming the wrong building, tell Campus Admin. They deactivate and repost.

Read **When to call 911** before you take a live queue.
