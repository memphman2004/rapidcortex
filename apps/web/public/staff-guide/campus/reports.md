# Reports and Clery tools (Campus)

**Reports** is campus analytics: counts, trends, and exports your role allows. It is not a 911 QA scorecard and it is not the live incident queue.

## Reports

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Filter to the campus site you need when the tenant has more than one.
3. Export only if your role allows it. Some roles are view only. If Export is missing, that is the permission, not a broken page.

Use reports for after-action and staffing. Do not publish student-identifiable detail without your counsel’s process.

## Clery tools

When Clery modules are on, Campus Admin and Supervisor may see:

- **Clery Report**
- **Review Queue**
- **Daily Crime Log**
- **Annual Security Report**
- **Compliance Dashboard**

NexCort iQ **does not file** the Annual Security Report or a Timely Warning. Those stay campus-owned legal processes. The product can hold review queues and drafts your coordinator already uses.

Counselors and faculty do not administer Clery. Dispatch does not file it from the incident queue.

If a number in a Clery queue disagrees with campus PD records, follow the Clery coordinator. Do not treat the software count as the filing.
