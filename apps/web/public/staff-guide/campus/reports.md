# Reports and Clery tools (Campus)

**Reports** is campus analytics — counts, trends, and exports your role allows. It is not a 911 QA scorecard.

## Reports

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Filter to the campus site you need.
3. Export only if your role allows it. Some roles are **view only**.

Use reports for after-action and staffing. Do not publish student-identifiable detail without counsel.

## Clery tools

When Clery modules are on, Campus Admin and Supervisor may see:

- **Clery Report**
- **Review Queue**
- **Daily Crime Log**
- **Annual Security Report**
- **Compliance Dashboard**

Rapid Cortex **does not file** your Annual Security Report or Timely Warning for you. Those remain campus-owned legal processes. The product can hold review queues and drafts.

Counselors and faculty do not administer Clery.

If a number in a Clery queue disagrees with campus PD records, follow your Clery coordinator — not the software count alone.
