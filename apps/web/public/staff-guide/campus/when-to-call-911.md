# When to call 911 (Campus)

NexCort iQ Campus Safety is **not** a 911 dispatch console. It does not replace campus police radio, telephony, or medical direction.

## Call 911 (or campus PD per your SOP) immediately when

- Violence or a credible threat is in progress
- Fire, smoke, or an alarm your SOP treats as an emergency
- A medical emergency, including unconsciousness or severe injury
- A missing child or at-risk person when SOP says emergency services must be notified
- Any situation your campus emergency plan labels as a public-safety emergency

Do not wait for a NexCort iQ incident to route itself to 911. **Make the voice call.** Then update the campus incident so other staff share the same picture.

## Use NexCort iQ Campus for

- Safety reports from students, faculty, and visitors, including QR and NFC posts
- Campus security and dispatch coordination
- Wellness reports that route to counseling
- Cameras and zone awareness
- Occupant alerts when that module is on

## Escalation in the product

Some roles can flag a campus incident toward 911. That flag is a **handoff record**. It does not write PSAP CAD and it does not place the 911 call.

Counselors: imminent harm follows counseling SOP, which often means calling 911 or campus PD. Closing the wellness item is not that call.

If you are unsure, call 911 and tell the campus supervisor what you did.
