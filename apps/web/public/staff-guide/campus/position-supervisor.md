# Campus Supervisor

You run live campus operations: incident oversight, QR/NFC, cameras, and escalation. This is a campus safety console, not a 911 floor.

## You can

- Monitor and update campus incidents across zones
- Create and deactivate QR / NFC codes (same manage permission as Admin)
- Use cameras, occupant alerts, EAP checklists, and war rooms when those features are on
- Escalate to 911 or department heads per SOP
- Run reports (Clery views may be view-only)

## You cannot

- Operate a PSAP CAD queue or approve 911 CAD write-back
- Manage Clery configuration the way Campus Admin does (module-dependent)

## Typical shift

1. Open the campus dashboard and incident list.
2. Watch QR/NFC inbound reports from students and visitors.
3. Assign security or dispatch follow-up.
4. If a situation meets emergency criteria, call 911 (or escalate in-product per SOP) and keep the campus record updated.
5. After events, deactivate temporary posts (tables, stages, closed halls).

## Locations

Supervisors can deactivate codes. Walk the grounds after you turn a location off so leftover stickers are removed.
