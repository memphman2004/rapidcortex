# Campus Supervisor

You run live campus operations: incident oversight, QR and NFC, cameras, and escalation. This is a campus safety console, not a 911 floor.

## You can

- Monitor and update campus incidents across zones
- Create and deactivate QR and NFC codes
- Use cameras, occupant alerts, and, when those features are on, EAP checklists and war rooms
- Escalate per SOP and keep the campus record aligned with the 911 call you already made
- Open reports. Some Clery views are view only

## You cannot

- Operate a PSAP CAD queue or approve 911 CAD write-back
- File Clery for the institution
- Give yourself counselor clinical notes outside SOP

## Typical shift

1. Open the dashboard and the incident list. Set the campus site if you are not covering all of them.
2. Watch QR and NFC reports as they arrive.
3. Assign security or dispatch follow-up.
4. If the situation is an emergency, call 911 (or campus PD per SOP) and then update the campus record.
5. After events, deactivate temporary posts: tables, stages, closed halls. Pull the physical sticker when you turn the code off.

## Locations

You have the same code-manage permission as Campus Admin. Walk the grounds after you deactivate a location so a leftover sign does not keep opening reports.
