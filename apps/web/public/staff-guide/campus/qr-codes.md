# QR Codes page (Campus)

Campus has two related tools. Both live under **QR Codes**.

## Named report codes (Field app)

These are the codes students and visitors scan or tap to open a report. The list matches Rapid Cortex Field.

1. Open **QR Codes**.
2. Review name, zone, scan counts, and NFC tap counts.
3. **Download PNG** to reprint a sign.
4. **Copy QR URL** or **Copy NFC URL** for vendors.
5. **NFC programming** shows how to write an NTAG213 in the Field app.
6. **Assign cameras** so a scan opens the cameras for that building or floor.
7. **Deactivate** if the post should stop working (manage roles only).

Campus Security is **view only** on this list. Admin and Supervisor can create and deactivate.

## Location QR (RCLI)

1. On **QR Codes**, open **Location QR (RCLI)**.
2. These are building and zone scan points with an RCLI identifier.
3. They are separate from named Field-app report codes.
4. **Deactivate** retires that location. It is not a hard delete.

## SMS numbers

If your campus uses a dedicated inbound SMS number, open **SMS numbers** from the QR Codes page. Cold inbound texts route by that number, not by a shared 911 line.
