# QR Codes page (Campus)

Campus **QR Codes** holds two kinds of posts. A scan or tap opens a campus report. It does not call 911.

## Who can change them

- **Campus Admin** and **Campus Supervisor** can create, download, assign cameras, and deactivate.
- **Campus Security** can view and download. Deactivate is not on that role.
- **Campus Dispatch, Counselor, and Faculty** do not manage codes. Dispatch does not have this page.

PSAP roles cannot manage campus QR or NFC.

## Named report codes (Field app)

These are the signs students and visitors scan or tap. The list matches the NexiQ Field app.

1. Open **QR Codes**.
2. Review the name, zone, scan count, and NFC tap count.
3. **Download PNG** to reprint a damaged sign.
4. **Copy QR URL** or **Copy NFC URL** if a print vendor needs the destination.
5. **NFC programming** is how an NTAG is written from the Field app. The phone writes the tag.
6. **Assign cameras** so a scan opens the cameras for that building or floor.
7. **Deactivate** when the post should stop (manage roles only). The row stays for audit.

There is no rename-in-place. Create a new code with the correct public name, post it, and deactivate the old one.

## Location QR (RCLI)

1. On **QR Codes**, open **Location QR (RCLI)**.
2. These are building and zone scan points with an RCLI identifier.
3. They are separate from named Field-app report codes.
4. **Deactivate** retires that location. It is not a hard delete.

## SMS numbers

If this campus uses a dedicated inbound SMS number, open **SMS numbers** from the QR Codes page. Cold inbound texts route by that number. They are not a shared 911 line.
