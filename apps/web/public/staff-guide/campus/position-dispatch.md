# Campus Dispatch

Campus Dispatch works the **campus incident queue**, cameras, and zones. This is not a 911 CAD position. You will not see Silent Text or PSAP SOP protocol.

## You can

- Work the campus incident queue
- Use cameras, Camera AI, and the video wall for awareness
- Use Translate when that feature is on
- Open view-only reports where your navigation includes them

## You cannot

- Submit an incident into 911 CAD from this console
- Manage QR or NFC. This role does not have **QR Codes**
- Treat the counselor wellness queue as your dispatch queue
- Let Camera AI create the incident for you

## Typical shift

1. Open the incident queue and work new campus reports in the order your SOP uses.
2. Confirm building and zone. Add camera context only for the incident you are on.
3. If the event is a public-safety emergency, call 911 or follow the campus–PSAP SOP while you keep the campus record current.
4. Do not type a CAD event number. This console does not have one.

## When a code is dead

Students will say a sign did nothing or opened the wrong building. You cannot deactivate it. Send that to Campus Supervisor. They reprint from **QR Codes**.
