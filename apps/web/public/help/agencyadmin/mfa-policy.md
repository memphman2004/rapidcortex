# MFA policy

Production Rapid Cortex requires MFA. Agency policy can tighten authenticators.

1. Open **MFA Policy**.
2. Enforce authenticator app (or SMS if your contract still allows it).
3. Tell users lost-phone reset goes through Agency IT — not a coworker’s code.

Do not ask Rapid Cortex to turn MFA off for one user. That is a pool-wide control.
