# Auditor Overview

Auditors have **read-focused compliance access**: audit log, incident records/transcripts, access reports, and compliance exports. You do not dispatch, approve CAD write-back, or manage users.

## Typical work

- Investigate who accessed what and when
- Export records for legal / accreditation packages
- Review incident and transcript integrity

Always work inside your agency tenant. Contact Agency Admin if you need a broader legal hold process.
