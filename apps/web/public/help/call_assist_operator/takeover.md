# Human Takeover

1. From the live session, choose **Take over** (or callback takeover when offered).
2. Greet the caller as a live agent for your agency — not as “the AI.”
3. Finish the non-emergency intake or transfer per SOP.
4. Document why takeover occurred: low confidence, caller request, emergency, or system fault.
5. If the Safety Engine already declared an emergency, prioritize the Connect transfer — do not resume the bot interview.

Takeover is expected. Fighting to keep the caller on the bot is an operational failure.
