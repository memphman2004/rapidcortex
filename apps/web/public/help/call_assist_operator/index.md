# Call Assist Operator Overview

Call Assist is **non-emergency AI intake only**. It is **not** a 911 dispatch console, not CAD, and not a replacement for telecommunicators.

## Your job

- Monitor live non-emergency AI sessions
- Take over / assist when the bot escalates or confidence drops
- Ensure emergency detection transfers the caller to the live answering point immediately
- Never continue a non-emergency interview after an emergency is declared

## Banner reminder

Every Call Assist page shows that this is not a 911 emergency dispatch system. Believe the banner.

## Shift start

1. Sign in at `/app/call-assist/operator` (or the URL Admin gave you).
2. Confirm the correct agency tenant.
3. Open **Live calls** and verify session health.
4. Know the emergency transfer destination for your city.
