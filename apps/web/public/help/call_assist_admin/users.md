# Call Assist Users

1. Open **Users**.
2. Invite only `call_assist_admin`, `call_assist_supervisor`, or `call_assist_operator`.
3. Send the Help panel link — unlimited article access for every seat.
4. Confirm post-login lands on `/app/call-assist/{role}`, never `/{jurisdiction}/dashboard`.
5. Deactivate the same day staff leave; do not share logins.

Assigning a PSAP `dispatcher` role to Call Assist-only staff will route them into the wrong product shell after MFA.
