# Platform Superadmin Overview

You hold immutable platform authority: agencies, feature flags, infrastructure, billing visibility, and (roadmap) time-boxed Grants. Use least privilege mentally even when the product allows more.

## Differences from RC Admin

- Feature flags at `/rc-admin/access`
- Grants (roadmap) — omitted from nav until shipped
- Cross-tenant tools without agency scoping exemptions abuse

Still: no PSAP live workspace widgets. No inventing customer references or enabling CAD write-back in production without go/no-go criteria.
