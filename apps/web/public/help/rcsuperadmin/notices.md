# Platform Notices

1. Open **Platform Notices**.
2. Draft a clear maintenance or incident message (impact, start, end, workaround).
3. Target one agency or all agencies as appropriate.
4. Send, then verify on a canary tenant when the change is risky.
5. Close or update the notice when the window ends — do not leave stale banners.

Notices are operational communications for agency admins — not marketing email.
