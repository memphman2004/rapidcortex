# Dispatcher Performance Reports

1. Open **Performance**.
2. Select individual or team scope and a fair date range (full shifts, not cherry-picked hours).
3. Read volume, handle time, and QA-linked metrics together.
4. Combine with supervisor coaching notes and wellness context before briefing command.
5. Export only through approved channels; do not publish rankings your agency policy forbids.

Software scores are assistive. They are not automatic discipline.
