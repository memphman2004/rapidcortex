# Sharing an incident

Cross-agency **incident shares** expose a controlled view of an incident to another NexCort iQ agency when your policy allows it.

## Before you share

Confirm SOP allows sharing this call (PII, tactical, or medical content).

## How to share

1. Open the incident.
2. Use **Share** (incident shares).
3. Choose the partner agency your admin has enabled.
4. Understand what the share exposes — typically status and narrative fields configured for your tenant, not your entire CAD.

Shares are audited. If you should not share, do not click it — radio/CAD mutual aid remains the operational path.
