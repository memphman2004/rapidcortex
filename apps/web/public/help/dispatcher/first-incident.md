# Creating your first incident

1. Sign in and land on your jurisdiction dashboard.
2. Confirm **Connections** shows the NexCort iQ API **live**.
3. Open or select an incident in the **incident queue** (left). For a new intake, follow your agency’s create-incident control — NexCort iQ does not invent a CAD event for you.
4. Verify **agency** and **title** match the call you are working.
5. Read the transcript. If **Original:** appears under English text, read both.
6. Open **Intelligence** and click **Refresh AI** once if you need a triage snapshot.
7. If CAD write-back is enabled for your agency **and** policy allows it, complete **CAD Entry** and submit only after you review the payload. Many agencies keep write-back off — then you copy into CAD yourself.

Do not use scripted demo playback on a live call. Academy playback is a separate demo path.
