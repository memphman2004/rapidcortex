# Caller Card & Premise Notes

The **Caller Card** pulls known context for the ANI/caller identity so you can see hazards and history without leaving the live workspace.

## What you will see

- Caller name / ANI when available from telephony
- Prior incident history for the same number or address (agency-scoped)
- Premise / hazard notes your agency has stored (weapons, oxygen, gate codes policy notes, etc.)
- Flags that supervisors or analysts marked as operationally relevant

## How to use it during a call

1. Select the incident so the Caller Card loads.
2. Scan hazard flags **before** you commit units or CAD comments that assume a quiet scene.
3. Mention relevant premise notes in your CAD narrative only when policy allows.
4. Do not read confidential wellness or counseling notes onto a shared radio channel.

## What Caller Card is not

- Not a criminal history system of record
- Not a substitute for CAD premise files your agency already maintains
- Not cross-tenant — you only see your agency's data

If the card is empty, treat the call as first-contact and continue normal intake. Empty does not mean "safe."
