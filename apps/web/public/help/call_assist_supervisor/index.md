# Call Assist Supervisor Overview

Supervisors oversee **live non-emergency sessions, QA, and floor relief** for Call Assist. You still are not on a 911 CAD console.

## Focus areas

- Live call monitoring and operator coaching
- QA review of AI + human assisted sessions
- Escalation when emergency transfer or Connect misbehaves
- Staffing the operator queue during surges

Use `/app/call-assist/supervisor` — do not redirect staff to `/{jurisdiction}/dashboard`.
