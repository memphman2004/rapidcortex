# Call Transfer & Takeover

Use transfer / takeover when a dispatcher needs relief, language support, or a supervisor must assume the call.

## Transfer between dispatchers

1. Confirm both seats are available and authenticated.
2. Initiate transfer per your telephony / Connect flow (NexCort iQ does not replace the CPE).
3. Brief the receiving dispatcher on nature, location, and open actions.
4. Confirm the incident ownership updates in the queue.

## Supervisor takeover

Take over only when policy allows and the floor needs it. Document the reason in notes. Monitoring or takeover events are auditable — do not "silently" sit on a call without the watching indicator when the product shows one.
