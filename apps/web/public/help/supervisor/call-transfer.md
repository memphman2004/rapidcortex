# Call transfer and takeover

1. Identify the incident and the dispatcher who owns it.
2. Use **Transfer** to move the Rapid Cortex workspace to another dispatcher, or **Takeover** when you will work the call.
3. Coordinate on radio/phone. The software does not replace telephony transfer.
4. Confirm the new owner sees the transcript and intelligence panel.

Document the transfer in incident notes if SOP requires it.
