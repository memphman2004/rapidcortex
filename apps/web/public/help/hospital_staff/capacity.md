# Updating Bed Status

1. Open **Capacity** for your department at shift start.
2. Enter available beds / status truthfully (holds, cleaning, diversion).
3. Save immediately after OR holds, discharges, or diversion changes.
4. Hand off ownership at shift change — do not leave stale green/red overnight.
5. If you cannot update, page Hospital Admin. Yesterday’s numbers are an operational risk for EMS routing partners.
